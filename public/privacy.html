Privacy policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

Northcroft is a private workspace for a small quantity surveying team. We use it to keep our tasks, projects, engineer's instructions, chats and files in one place. It's invite-only: you can only sign in if the team manager has added you. Mohamed Hassan builds and runs the app.

This page explains what information the app keeps, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. If anything here isn't clear, email rabbi6y7@gmail.com.

What the app keeps

  • Your account: your name, email address and your role in the team.
  • The work you add: tasks, sub tasks, projects, EIs, comments, chat messages and announcements.
  • Files you attach: documents, drawings and photos you add to a task, project or chat.
  • Voice notes: if you record one, we keep the recording and a written version of it.
  • Notifications: if you turn on push notifications, we keep the details your browser gives us so we can send them to your device.
  • Basic technical logs: our hosting providers keep short-lived logs (for example, the time of a request) to keep the service running and secure.

We don't use advertising, we don't use tracking cookies, and we don't sell or rent anyone's information.

Google Drive

Files the team attaches are stored in one Google Drive account that the team manager connects to the app. When the manager connects it, Google asks for permission to "see, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app" (the drive.file permission). That means:

  • The app can only see and manage the files and folders it created itself, which live in a "Northcroft" folder. It can't see anything else in that Drive.
  • We use this access only to save the files team members attach, to open them for signed-in team members, and to move a file to the Drive bin when someone deletes it in the app.
  • Files aren't shared publicly. Team members open them through short links that expire after an hour, and files in a private chat can only be opened by the people in that chat.
  • If a team member pastes a Drive link that is already shared as "Anyone with the link", the app reads that file's name so it can show it. It doesn't copy or change the file.

Northcroft's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In plain terms: we only use Google data to provide the features described above. We don't use it for advertising, we don't sell it, we don't let people read it except to provide the feature, keep it secure or meet the law, and we don't use it to train AI models.

The Drive owner can disconnect the app at any time, either from the app's settings or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting stops the app's access straight away. Files already saved stay in that Drive.

Services that help run the app

A few trusted providers process information on our behalf, only as much as each feature needs:

  • Supabase stores the database, sign-in details and voice recordings.
  • Vercel hosts the website.
  • Google Drive stores attached files, as described above.
  • Groq turns voice notes into text.
  • Anthropic tidies that text into clear English and translates notes recorded in other languages.

Information from Google Drive is not sent to Groq or Anthropic.

How long we keep it

We keep information for as long as the team uses the app. When someone leaves the team, the manager can switch off their account. You can ask us to delete your information at any time, and we'll do it unless we need to keep a work record for a genuine business or legal reason. When a file is deleted in the app, it goes to the Drive bin, which Google empties after 30 days.

Your choices

Email rabbi6y7@gmail.com if you'd like a copy of your information, want something corrected, or want it deleted. We'll reply within 30 days.

Keeping it safe

You have to sign in to see anything. Information travels over encrypted connections, file links expire after an hour, and access is limited to the people in the team.

Changes

If we change how the app uses information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
