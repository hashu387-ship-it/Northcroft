Northcroft

A private workspace for our quantity surveying team.

Northcroft keeps the team's day-to-day work in one place, so nothing gets lost between emails, chats and shared folders. It's invite-only: team members sign in with the account the manager set up for them.

Open the app

What it does

  • Tasks and sub tasks, each with an owner, a due date and a status.
  • Projects, with a register of engineer's instructions (EIs) and their status: prepared, submitted, on hold and so on.
  • Team chats and announcements, including voice notes that are written out as text.
  • File attachments. Files are saved in the team's Google Drive, inside a "Northcroft" folder, and open for signed-in team members only.

How it uses Google Drive

The team manager connects one Google Drive account. The app asks only for permission to work with the files it creates itself, so it can't see anything else in that Drive. Read the details in our privacy policy.
